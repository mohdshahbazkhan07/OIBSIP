Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan

Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan was a Pakistani musician, primarily a singer of qawwali, a devotional music of the Sufis. He is considered one of the greatest voices ever recorded and is credited with introducing qawwali music to international audiences.

Nusrat was born on October 13, 1948, in Faisalabad, Pakistan. He came from a family of musicians and was trained in the art of qawwali by his father and uncle. He began performing at a young age and quickly gained recognition for his powerful voice and emotive performances.

Throughout his career, Nusrat released numerous albums and collaborated with various artists from different genres. He was known for his ability to blend traditional qawwali with contemporary music, which helped him reach a wider audience. Some of his most famous songs include "Afreen Afreen," "Tere Bin Nahin Lagda," and "Mast Qalandar."

Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan passed away on August 16, 1997, but his legacy continues to live on through his music. He is remembered as a legendary figure in the world of music and is celebrated for his contributions to the art of qawwali.
